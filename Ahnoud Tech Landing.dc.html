(FAQ)

Common questions.

What does Ahnoud Tech do?

Ahnoud Tech is a design and engineering studio. We take a company from brand identity and web design through web development, AI agents and automation, to the infrastructure that keeps it running.

Where is Ahnoud Tech based?

Our studio is at Building 14, 18 Street - Zaa'beel Second - Za'abeel - Dubai - UAE. We work with clients worldwide, in English and Arabic.

What is Kinomad Studio?

Kinomad Studio is our in-house team. It covers brand identity, web design, 3D visualization & animation, web development, AI agents & automation, and application DevOps.

Can one studio handle both design and development?

Yes. Design and engineering sit in one team, so a project moves from identity to launch and ongoing operations without changing vendors.

Do you support a product after launch?

Yes. Application DevOps covers deployment, infrastructure, and care after launch, so what we ship keeps running.

How do I start a project?

Use the Start a project button, email hello@ahnoud.com, or message us on WhatsApp or Telegram at +971 50 483 9038 with your goals, scope, and timing. We reply within two working days.
